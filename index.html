TPMS Sensor Programming

It's usually the sensor, not the tire.

Every wheel has a pressure sensor inside it. When one stops reporting, the light comes on and stays on even with all four tires set correctly.

  • Sensor batteries are sealed and die at around seven to ten years. There's no charging them.
  • After a rotation or a wheel swap the system often just needs a relearn, because it no longer knows which sensor sits at which corner.
  • A second set of winter wheels needs its own sensors, and programming every time it goes on.
  • Sensors also get broken during careless mounting.

We read all four, tell you which one is actually the problem, and program the system back. If the light is on because a tire is genuinely low, the free air check sorts it in five minutes and you owe us nothing.
