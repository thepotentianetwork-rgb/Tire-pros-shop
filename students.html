First winter in Logan?

Cache Valley winters are hard on tires.

If you're from somewhere warm, Logan's first cold snap can be a surprise. Here's what catches new students out, and how we sort it.

  • Tire light on after a cold night Free air check
  • Light stays on with good pressure TPMS programming
  • Bald tires before winter Used or new tires
  • Snow tires on, and off in spring Seasonal swap
  • Curb, pothole or canyon damage Inspection

Cold air drops tire pressure, about 1 PSI for every 10°F, so the tire pressure light often comes on the first frosty morning. Pull in and we'll set all four for free. Studded tires have to come off Utah roads by March 31.

Questions from USU students

Before you come in.

How far are you from USU?

We're at 1704 S 800 W #4 on the south end of Logan, off 600 West and South Highway 89. It's a short drive from Utah State's Logan campus.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in and we'll fit you in when a bay opens. If you'd rather not wait, book a time online or text us.

How much is a tire mount and balance?

From $25 per tire. We'll mount tires you bought online too. Everything else is quoted before we start.

Do you sell cheap used tires?

Yes. We keep inspected used car, truck and SUV tires on the rack. We measure the tread and read the age code, so you know what you're buying. Stock changes weekly, so call or text to check your size.

My tire pressure light came on when it got cold. What do I do?

It's usually the cold dropping your pressure. Come in for a free air check. If the light stays on with all four tires set correctly, it's a TPMS sensor, and we can read and program them.

Are you open on weekends?

We're open Saturdays, 8 AM to 6 PM, same as weekdays. Closed Sundays.
